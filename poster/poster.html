Same person or not?

AUC is 98.6–98.8 for all 4 losses. The gap shows at strict thresholds: at 1 false accept in 1,000, ArcFace accepts 81.7% of true pairs, Triplet 52.9%.

Take-home

  • Training on faces is the big step: the weakest loss (43.4) beats the best classical method (13.0) by 30 points.
  • The loss still matters: 43.4 → 73.8 with everything else identical.
  • Tight is not enough: Contrastive builds the tightest groups but crowds people together; the two best losses spread people over the whole sphere.
